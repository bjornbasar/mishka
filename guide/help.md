# Help

Mishka ships an in-app guide covering the same ground as this site, one
click away inside the app itself — handy when you're already signed in
and want an answer without leaving the page you're on.

A small internal endpoint keeps your session's form-submission token
fresh in the background — nothing you'll ever need to visit directly, but
it's part of what keeps long-open tabs from hitting a stale-session error
on your next click.

## Routes

| Route |
|---|
| `/help` |
| `/csrf-token` |
