# Mishka Den

Mishka Den is a quiet command-centre for the household — calendar, chores,
health tracking, and the everyday coordination a family needs, in one
place.

## The mental model (read this first)

Three things everything else depends on:

1. **One Mishka can host many households.** Each family is its own
   household — a completely separate calendar, chores and member list. You
   never see anyone else's data.
2. **You can belong to more than one household.** A divorced parent, a
   foster carer, a live-in nanny — any of these can be a member of more
   than one household and switch between them with one click.
3. **Every household has exactly one owner** — the person who created it.
   The owner can rename it, transfer ownership, remove members, regenerate
   the invite code, or delete the whole thing. Members can do everything
   else (add chores, edit events, mark things done) but can't remove people
   or delete the household.
